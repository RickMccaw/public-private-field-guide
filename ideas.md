# Visual Direction — The Collapse of the Public–Private Divide

## Three Approaches Considered

### Theme Name: Field Notes After Dark
**Very Brief Intro:** A documentary field-guide experience that brings the paper’s civic stakes into focus through warm investigative photography, archival paper textures, and precise editorial typography. It should feel like a special report assembled from observation, records, and public evidence.
**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: The Civic Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A restrained institutional archive aesthetic, pairing broad white margins with black filing tabs, deep blue data marks, and typographic systems reminiscent of court records and public reports. It foregrounds legal structure over cinematic mood.
**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Signal / Silence
**Very Brief Intro:** A high-contrast interactive essay where darkness represents ambient capture and quiet paper-like surfaces represent protected personal space. It is abstract, contemplative, and uses motion to reveal the accumulation of data traces.
**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Approach: Field Notes After Dark

### Design Movement
**Editorial photojournalism fused with contemporary longform investigative reporting.** The experience takes cues from serious magazine dossiers, public-interest newsrooms, and the graphic restraint of field manuals rather than a conventional technology product site.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence before spectacle:** every visual moment should make the surveillance thesis clearer, not turn the subject into generic cyberpunk.
2. **Human scale within systems scale:** individual scenes, hands, doors, faces, roads, and classrooms make abstract infrastructure legible.
3. **Deliberate contrast:** use dense, shadowed photography against warm paper and strong typography to distinguish ambient capture from civic agency.
4. **Structured investigation:** the 26 chapters appear as an accessible research index, not as a flat wall of content.

### Color Philosophy
The palette treats **ink blue-black** as the atmosphere of persistent systems and **warm ivory** as the protected space of careful inquiry. A singular **signal gold** does not decorate; it marks the line of attention—chapter numbers, important thresholds, and active navigation. Muted rust introduces urgency only at points of friction or consequence.

### Layout Paradigm
The homepage behaves like a **vertical field dossier**. A narrow fixed editorial rail carries navigation and reading position; wide visual panels break into asymmetric investigation cards and chapter index fragments. The page never relies on a centered marketing stack—images interrupt, crop, and bleed against a disciplined reading column.

### Signature Elements
1. A **gold signal line** that tracks the reader’s place in the report.
2. **Black news-crawl bands** with concise explanatory statements below major visual panels.
3. A **case-file index** where chapter numbers act as navigational evidence markers rather than generic cards.

### Interaction Philosophy
Scrolling should feel like opening a field dossier: chapters reveal in short, calm sequences and navigation gives immediate orientation. Clicks move to evidence, questions, and chapters without theatrical delays; the interface privileges reading, revisiting, and collecting a clear line of inquiry.

### Animation
Use subtle 180–260ms opacity and translate transitions for entries, buttons, and chapter rows. The signal line advances with scroll position, image panels receive a restrained parallax shift only when reduced motion is not requested, and all animation stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for high-stakes headlines and **IBM Plex Sans** for body, labels, and navigation. Chapter numbers use a compact mono-style treatment through IBM Plex Mono; headlines are large, editorial, and occasionally italicized, while body text stays highly readable at a generous line height.

### Brand Essence
**A visual field guide for students and citizens mapping how ordinary data becomes surveillance power.** Precise, watchful, civic-minded.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct, specific, and investigative; microcopy invites a question rather than selling a feature. Example lines: “A camera sees a moment. A system remembers a life.” and “Follow the data before it follows you.” Generic welcome language and empty startup phrasing are not permitted.

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a **split aperture / open eye**: a black circular lens bisected by a narrow gold horizon line, suggesting both observation and the right to look back. The wordmark pairs the abbreviated title **PUBLIC / PRIVATE** with a sharp dividing slash and is never simply set as unmodified default text.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Gold — #E8A33D.** This is the unmistakable indicator of attention, evidence, and reader progress throughout the site.

## Style Decisions

- **Signal Gold #E8A33D** is reserved for reader orientation, evidence markers, active states, chapter numerals, and threshold lines; it does not dominate large background surfaces.
- The **split aperture / open-eye mark** recurs in the header, footer, and evidence bands as a visible dossier instrument rather than a minor decorative logo.
- Research navigation uses **case-file and archive language**—evidence tabs, filing marks, index rows, and threshold bands—instead of rounded SaaS-style filter chips.
- Black **evidence bands** punctuate key transitions with concise, news-crawl-like statements that make the report’s central claims memorable.
